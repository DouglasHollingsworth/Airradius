THE BUSINESS / THE PURPOSE

A clearer picture.

AirRadius is developing software for airspace awareness and incident coordination.

Our goal is to help teams responsible for places and communities bring scattered observations into a shared view—and keep the next decision clear.

An operator’s perspective.
A builder’s mindset.

Douglas Hollingsworth is a veteran, entrepreneur, and inventor. His operations background taught him to look beyond individual pieces of information and understand how they fit together.

That perspective shapes AirRadius: useful technology should help people understand the situation, set priorities, and work together.

He brings a hands-on discipline to that ambition: build a practical version, test the assumptions, and improve it with evidence.

02 / FROM PERSPECTIVE TO PRACTICE

One picture.
A shared way forward.

This mindset becomes a simple operating philosophy: understand what you see, coordinate what happens next, and learn from the outcome.

01

Understand the situation.

Bring observations and their context together. Keep sources and uncertainty visible, so people can assess what they know.

02

Coordinate the next step.

Give teams a shared view, a clear handoff, and responsibility for what happens next.

03

Learn from the outcome.

Preserve events and decisions for replay and review. Use that history to improve the next response.
