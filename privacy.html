Privacy and demonstration use

This presentation uses fictional observations. The demo stores its fictional case history in this browser only. Reset clears that demo state. Avoid entering personal, customer, or operational information.

No analytics, marketing trackers, contact form, live sensor connections, or external model requests are enabled by this application. The hosting provider may process standard request logs under its own policies.

AirRadius is a development alpha. Demonstration outputs support review and do not establish identity, hostile intent, detection performance, or authority to mitigate aircraft. This text is a draft for owner review.

The pilot scoping tool uses non-sensitive choices in memory on your device to prepare a planning brief. It does not submit a lead or upload choices to AirRadius. Downloaded files stay under your control. If a public business email is configured, opening an email draft uses your own email application and requires you to review and send it.
