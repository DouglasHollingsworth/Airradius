AIRRADIUS / DRAFT NOTICE FOR OWNER REVIEW

Privacy and fictional training use.

Use fictional training data only. Do not supply personal details, credentials, customer records, private CEO/pilot information or live operational data. This notice describes the current preview; it is not a claim of legal compliance.

Public presentation and browser demo

The browser demo uses fictional observations and stores the current exercise case/history in your browser's local storage. Its workflow audit is session memory. Reset replaces the stored exercise with its fictional fixture and retains the current session run audit. Your browser's site-data controls can clear local storage.

The motion layer stores only your paused-motion preference locally under airradius.motion.paused. It also respects your operating system's reduced-motion preference. No application analytics, marketing trackers, contact form, live sensors or external model requests are enabled.

Pilot scoping and exports

The pilot scoping page uses non-sensitive selectors in page memory to prepare a nonbinding planning brief. It does not submit a lead or upload those choices to AirRadius. Downloaded files stay under your control. If a verified public email or booking URL is configured later, opening that channel uses your own email client or an external booking service; no automatic message is sent.

Bounded MCP training presets

The public MCP tools accept a scenario selector for one of two fixed fictional presets: duplicate-and-delay or separated-observations. They do not accept arbitrary record uploads, private case IDs, free-text notes, credentials or live feeds. Deterministic handlers compute fictional outputs with run state, evidence references and limitations.

The training application does not persist MCP request payloads in an application database or disk-backed payload store. Transient computation and in-memory run metadata may exist during execution. The hosting provider and the client/platform you use may process request or connection logs under their own policies. Their logging and retention practices are separate; this preview does not promise a specific log-retention or deletion period.

External services and support

GitHub issue reports and any MCP client you choose are handled by those services. Public issues may be visible to others. Do not post private or personal material. This application has no confirmed dedicated support mailbox at present; see the support page for the available public issue route.

Capability limits

Demonstration outputs do not establish identity, hostile intent, detection performance, mitigation authority or operational readiness. AirRadius does not access private records through this training preview.
