Consulting

From operational gaps to a practical plan.

Our initial advisory offer focuses on information systems and protective operations workflows—without implying investigative, guarding, or counter-drone mitigation services.

01 / Discover

Technology readiness review

Inventory current observation sources, reporting tools, operational dependencies, and data gaps. Map what equipment could integrate later.

02 / Design

Incident workflow assessment

Review how reports are received, verified, prioritized, assigned, escalated, and closed. Recommend clear ownership and human-approved action paths.

03 / Demonstrate

Pilot design & evaluation

Build a limited exercise plan with acceptance criteria, privacy controls, source provenance, and an after-action evaluation using clearly labeled test data.

AirRadius Digital MOC

Awareness. Intelligence. Response.

A common operating workflow designed to turn scattered observations into understandable, traceable decisions.

Pillar 01

Awareness

Map observations and their sources. Preserve time, location, limitations, and a common operational picture.

Pillar 02

Intelligence

Connect relevant context, distinguish facts from inferences, and keep unknowns visible.

Pillar 03

Response

Guide human-authorized escalation, incident ownership, communication, and after-action documentation.

Current Digital MOC capabilities are demonstrated primarily with simulated inputs and browser-local records. Real sensor feeds, secure shared production records, and field performance have not yet been validated.
