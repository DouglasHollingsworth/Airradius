# AirRadius Training reference

This knowledge file describes two fixed fictional exercises. It contains no private business or operator records. Actions return the actual fixture and result so evidence can be checked directly.

`duplicate-and-delay`: four fictional observations include a same-source same-time duplicate candidate, a nearby optical observation with delayed ingestion, and an older separated observation. One fictional review note exists. Identity is unknown. Correlation uses a time gap of at most ten seconds and distance of at most ten exercise units; those rules do not establish identity or a calibrated probability.

`separated-observations`: two same-time observations are far apart in exercise units. Correlation must not falsely merge them. Ingestion timestamps remain distinct from event timestamps.

Correlation and handoff mode is SIMULATED. Replay mode is REPLAY and retains the same fictional fixture provenance. Backend execution is DETERMINISTIC. No actual sensor, external model, authenticated operational owner, mitigation equipment or customer data is connected.

A run reports QUEUED, RUNNING and a terminal state. Successful outputs include source references, facts, inferences, limitations, draft recommendations and one request-only audit. Outputs do not remain in application storage after that invocation. The hosting provider and ChatGPT may retain platform/request/conversation logs under their own policies; publisher log-retention configuration still needs confirmation before marketplace submission.

Do not upload evidence or secrets. Do not claim that these exercises measure real detection performance. A training draft is not an accepted handoff, a live operation or an authenticated multi-user collaboration record.
